Their story

Every love story is special.

This one just happens to be their favorite.

Chapter one

How they met

The story starts at VCU, with a boy who was too nervous to say hello.

Shafi first noticed Phoenix a full semester before they ever spoke. He saw her around campus again and again, and every time, he lost his nerve. The semester ended, and he figured he had missed his chance.

Fate had other plans. When the next semester began, the two of them somehow had the exact same class schedule. Every single class, side by side.

On the first day, Shafi was wandering the halls, hopelessly lost, searching for a math class. And there she was, wandering the very same hallway, looking just as lost. This time he didn’t let the moment pass. He took a breath, walked over, and asked, “Are you looking for the math class too?”

She was. They found the classroom together, and they have been finding their way together ever since.

After that, they were inseparable. Before the month was over they were dating, spending nearly every day of the week together, and Shafi had already met Phoenix’s parents. When you know, you know.

Chapter two

Valentine’s Day, 2025

It was, quite simply, the most wonderful day of their lives, and they didn’t even plan it.

They had both taken the day off work just to spend Valentine’s Day together. The weather was beautiful. Somewhere in the middle of an easy morning conversation, one thought led to another, and they looked at each other and said: let’s get married today.

So they did. They put on their fanciest clothes, drove out to the courthouse, and promised each other forever. Then they celebrated the only way that felt right: with an amazing steak dinner, just the two of them, grinning at each other across the table like they had gotten away with something.

No guest list, no timeline, no stress. Just a beautiful day and the person each of them loves most. To them, it was nothing short of spectacular.

Chapter three

Why now

Because they finally figured out how they wanted to celebrate.

As most of you know, Phoenix’s parents are Catholic and Shafi’s parents are Muslim. They love where they each come from, and for a long time they went back and forth trying to imagine a wedding that would feel right for everyone. The more they planned, the more complicated, expensive, and overwhelming it became.

Then they realized they had been overthinking it. What they wanted was never the venue, the traditions, or the perfect program. What they wanted was all of you, together.

So they let the rest go. A spring afternoon in the park, good barbecue on the grill, and the people they love all in one place. That’s their idea of a perfect celebration. They hope it feels like one to you, too.
