The announcement

Surprise! They’re married.

Shafi and Phoenix have some news, and they have been holding onto it until they could share it with everyone at once.

On Valentine’s Day 2025, they walked into a courthouse holding hands and walked out husband and wife. There was no big crowd and no grand production. It was just the two of them, a promise, and the kind of happiness that makes your face hurt from smiling. It was small and quiet, and it was exactly right for them.

But something was missing: all of you.

A marriage isn’t only about two people. It’s about the families who raised them, the people who shaped them, and everyone who has loved them along the way. You are the reason they are who they are, and the reason they found their way to each other. It never felt right to keep celebrating without you.

So they are finally doing the part they skipped. They’re gathering everyone they love in one place, both families side by side, to eat, laugh, and celebrate the best decision they ever made.

There’s no aisle, no seating chart, and no long speeches to sit through. Just good food, an afternoon in the park, and the people who matter most to them, together at last.

They love you, and they can’t wait to see you there.
