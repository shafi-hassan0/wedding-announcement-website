Good to know

The little details, so all you have to do is show up.

What should I wear?

Whatever makes you comfortable. Truly. Jeans, shorts, sneakers, anything goes. It’s a park, and Shafi and Phoenix would rather see you relaxed and happy than dressed up. You might want to bring a jacket, though; March can’t make up its mind.

Is this a wedding ceremony?

Nope, they already took care of that part. This is just the fun part: the celebration.

Can I bring the kids?

Please do! This is a family day through and through, and there’s plenty of room to run around.

What should I bring?

Just yourself and a good appetite. The food and drinks are covered, and the shelter has plenty of seating.

Where do I park?

There’s a free parking lot right at the park, so you can pull in and walk straight over to the shelter.

What if it rains?

The shelter is covered, so a few clouds won’t stop the party. A little rain never stopped a good love story. If the weather truly refuses to cooperate, the celebration will move to the following Saturday, March 20, with Saturday, March 27 as a second backup. Shafi and Phoenix will let everyone know ahead of time if the date changes.
