## 2. The Announcement

*(Photo 2: `web/static/photos/cutestpic.jpg`, beside the text: the close-up portrait of the two of them in pink and scarves.)*

**Surprise! They're married.**

Shafi and Phoenix have some news, and they have been holding onto it until they could share it with everyone at once.

On Valentine's Day 2025, they walked into a courthouse holding hands and walked out husband and wife. There was no big crowd and no grand production. It was just the two of them, a promise, and the kind of happiness that makes your face hurt from smiling. It was small and quiet, and it was exactly right for them.

But something was missing: all of you.

A marriage isn't only about two people. It's about the families who raised them, the people who shaped them, and everyone who has loved them along the way. You are the reason they are who they are, and the reason they found their way to each other. It never felt right to keep celebrating without you.

So they are finally doing the part they skipped. They're gathering everyone they love in one place, both families side by side, to eat, laugh, and celebrate the best decision they ever made.

There's no aisle, no seating chart, and no long speeches to sit through. Just good food, an afternoon in the park, and the people who matter most to them, together at last.

They love you, and they can't wait to see you there.

## 3. Their Story

*Every love story is special. This one just happens to be their favorite.*

**How they met**

*(Photo 3: `web/static/photos/early-selfie.jpg`: the early selfie with their heads together.)*

The story starts at VCU, with a boy who was too nervous to say hello.

Shafi first noticed Phoenix a full semester before they ever spoke. He saw her around campus again and again, and every time, he lost his nerve. The semester ended, and he figured he had missed his chance.

Fate had other plans. When the next semester began, the two of them somehow had the exact same class schedule. Every single class, side by side.

On the first day, Shafi was wandering the halls, hopelessly lost, searching for a math class. And there she was, wandering the very same hallway, looking just as lost. This time he didn't let the moment pass. He took a breath, walked over, and asked, "Are you looking for the math class too?"

She was. They found the classroom together, and they have been finding their way together ever since.

After that, they were inseparable. Before the month was over they were dating, spending nearly every day of the week together, and Shafi had already met Phoenix's parents. When you know, you know.

**Valentine's Day, 2025**

*(Photo 4: `web/static/photos/courthouse.jpeg`: the courthouse day itself, the two of them facing each other outside the courthouse in the sun, with flowers.)*

It was, quite simply, the most wonderful day of their lives, and they didn't even plan it.

They had both taken the day off work just to spend Valentine's Day together. The weather was beautiful. Somewhere in the middle of an easy morning conversation, one thought led to another, and they looked at each other and said: *let's get married today.*

So they did. They put on their fanciest clothes, drove out to the courthouse, and promised each other forever. Then they celebrated the only way that felt right: with an amazing steak dinner, just the two of them, grinning at each other across the table like they had gotten away with something.

No guest list, no timeline, no stress. Just a beautiful day and the person each of them loves most. To them, it was nothing short of spectacular.

**Why now**

*(Photo 5: `web/static/photos/restaurant-selfie.jpg`: the wide restaurant selfie, with the ring showing.)*

Because they finally figured out how they wanted to celebrate.

As most of you know, Phoenix's parents are Catholic and Shafi's parents are Muslim. They love where they each come from, and for a long time they went back and forth trying to imagine a wedding that would feel right for everyone. The more they planned, the more complicated, expensive, and overwhelming it became.

Then they realized they had been overthinking it. What they wanted was never the venue, the traditions, or the perfect program. What they wanted was all of you, together.

So they let the rest go. A spring afternoon in the park, good barbecue on the grill, and the people they love all in one place. That's their idea of a perfect celebration. They hope it feels like one to you, too.

## 8. Good to Know

*The little details, so all you have to do is show up.*

**What should I wear?**
Whatever makes you comfortable. Truly. Jeans, shorts, sneakers, anything goes. It's a park, and Shafi and Phoenix would rather see you relaxed and happy than dressed up. You might want to bring a jacket, though; March can't make up its mind.

**Is this a wedding ceremony?**
Nope, they already took care of that part. This is just the fun part: the celebration.

**Can I bring the kids?**
Please do! This is a family day through and through, and there's plenty of room to run around.

**What should I bring?**
Just yourself and a good appetite. The food and drinks are covered, and the shelter has plenty of seating.

**Where do I park?**
There's a free parking lot right at the park, so you can pull in and walk straight over to the shelter.

**What if it rains?**
The shelter is covered, so a few clouds won't stop the party. A little rain never stopped a good love story. If the weather truly refuses to cooperate, the celebration will move to the following Saturday, March 20, with Saturday, March 27 as a second backup. Shafi and Phoenix will let everyone know ahead of time if the date changes.
